LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Applies to the Filo app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The short version

  • Your documents, scans and signatures stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There’s no Filo account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t sell or share your data for advertising.
  • To manage purchases, our subscription provider receives an anonymous ID and your purchase history. That’s it.

1. Who we are

Filo is made by Ovidiu-Andrei Toma, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the limited personal data described below. You can contact us at ovidiutoma.app+filo@gmail.com.

2. Your documents stay on your device

Everything you open, import, scan, sign or edit in Filo is stored and processed locally on your device. This includes file contents, file names, folders, scans, recognized text, annotations and signatures. Scanning, text recognition (OCR), compression and conversion all run on your device. We have no servers that receive your documents, and we cannot see them. Your documents leave your device only when you choose: when you share or export them, or when you turn on iCloud sync.

3. iCloud sync (optional, Filo Pro)

If you turn on sync, Filo stores your documents in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices. This data is held by Apple under your Apple ID and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access your private iCloud data. Documents in your vault are never synced. You can turn sync off at any time in Filo’s Settings.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never receive your payment card details. To check whether you have access to Filo Pro, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by Filo;
  • your purchase and subscription history, such as products, dates, renewals, trials and refunds;
  • basic technical information: app version, device model, operating system version, App Store country and currency, and the IP address your device connects from.

We use this only to provide and restore your purchases and to understand subscription revenue. The legal basis is performance of our contract with you. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5. Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don’t identify you and don’t include your documents. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements (on Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements).

6. Device permissions

Filo asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it. You can change these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings.

PermissionUsed for
CameraScanning documents on iPhone and iPad. Images stay on your device.
PhotosFilo uses Apple’s photo picker, so it receives only the photos you choose. It doesn’t ask for access to your photo library.
Face ID / Touch IDLocking the app or your vault. Apple handles the face or fingerprint match; Filo never receives biometric data. Your device passcode (or Mac password) always works too.
Files you chooseImporting the files you pick in Files or Finder: Filo copies them into its own library and can’t see anything else.

7. What we don’t do

  • We don’t use advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell or rent personal data, or share it for cross-app tracking.
  • We don’t build profiles about you or read your documents to train any model.

8. How long data is kept

Documents on your device are kept until you delete them or delete the app. Synced documents stay in your iCloud until you delete them in Filo or in Settings › [your name] › iCloud (on Mac: System Settings › [your name] › iCloud). Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal and tax obligations.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold almost no data about you, the main thing we can act on is your purchase record. Email ovidiutoma.app+filo@gmail.com and include the order ID of your Filo purchase (it’s on Apple’s receipt email and at reportaproblem.apple.com), so we can find your purchase record.

10. International transfers

RevenueCat may process purchase data in the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children

Filo is a general-audience productivity app and isn’t directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Filo handles data, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, we’ll also let you know in the app.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: ovidiutoma.app+filo@gmail.com · Ovidiu-Andrei Toma.
